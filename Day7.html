Directions

  1. Cut: Slice the potatoes into even 1/4-inch to 1/2-inch sticks.
  2. Soak: Submerge the cut potatoes in a bowl of ice water for 30–60 minutes to remove excess starch (this prevents them from burning and keeps them crispy).
  3. Dry: Drain the potatoes completely and pat them entirely dry using a clean kitchen towel or paper towels.
  4. First Fry (Blanch): Heat your oil in a heavy-bottomed pot or Deep Fryer to 325°F (162°C). Fry the potatoes in small batches for 4–5 minutes until they are soft and pale blonde, but not browned. Remove and drain on paper towels.
  5. Second Fry (Crisp): Increase the oil temperature to 375°F (190°C). Drop the blanched fries back in (again in small batches) and fry for 2–3 minutes until they turn golden brown and crispy.
  6. Drain & Season: Remove the fries, drain excess oil on paper towels, and toss immediately with salt while they are hot.
