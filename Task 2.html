Bhagavad gita

chapter 2 -Verse 47

  • On the battlefield of Kurukshetra, Arjuna became confused and refused to fight because he saw his relatives, teachers, and friends standing on both sides. He felt sorrow and fear, believing that fighting them would bring only pain and destruction. At that moment, Lord Krishna advised Arjuna that as a warrior, it was his duty to protect righteousness. Krishna explained that Arjuna should perform his duty sincerely without worrying about whether he would win or lose.

  • Through this teaching, Krishna explained that people can control only their actions, not the results.Success and failure depend on many factors beyond our control. Therefore, we should focus on giving our best effort with honesty and dedication instead of becoming attached to the outcome. This verse teaches us to work with commitment, remain calm in every situation, and accept the results with a balanced mind.


    • Do your Duty Without Worrying About Results ~ Lord Krishna to Arjuna
